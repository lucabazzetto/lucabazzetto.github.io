Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This is a personal website. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, shows no ads and does not track you across other websites. I cannot identify individual visitors.

Who is responsible

Luca Bazzetto is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: luca.bazzetto@gmail.com.

Hosting: GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. To deliver the pages and keep the service secure, GitHub processes technical data about your request, including your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement, which also covers international data transfers.

Visitor statistics: GoatCounter

I use GoatCounter to count visits in aggregate. It records:

  • the page visited and the referring website or link tag (for example ?campaign=linkedin)
  • browser, operating system, screen width and language
  • approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address
  • clicks on links such as the CV download

GoatCounter does not store IP addresses, the full user agent or any identifier, and uses no cookies or browser storage. To avoid counting repeat visits twice, it keeps a short-lived session in memory for up to 8 hours. GoatCounter is operated by Martin Tournoij (Ireland) and stores data on servers in Germany and Finland. See the GoatCounter privacy policy.

Snowboard video

The video is hosted on this site and only loads when you open it. No third-party video player is used.

Fonts

Fonts are served from this site. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other font service.

Legal basis and retention

Hosting and aggregate statistics are based on my legitimate interest in running this website securely and knowing how many people visit it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Aggregate statistics contain no personal data and are kept while the site exists. GitHub keeps request data according to its own policy.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to it (Articles 15–21 GDPR). Because the statistics hold no identifiers, I usually cannot link them to you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live, work or where you think the infringement took place.
