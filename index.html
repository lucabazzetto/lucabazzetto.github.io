About me

I'm a Data Engineer at Electrolux Professional Group, where I started as an intern in 2025. I integrate data from many business systems into one governed analytics platform, and build the data products on top of it that Finance, Supply Chain, Manufacturing, HR and Operations use.

The part I enjoy most is where data engineering meets graphs and AI: modeling products and their relationships as property graphs, and preparing the datasets behind semantic search and AI initiatives. I studied Computer Science in Udine, spent an Erasmus year in Krakow, and I'm finishing an MSc in Data Science in Milan.

Off-screen, you can usually find me or over-engineering Docker containers on my self-hosted Raspberry Pi home servers.
